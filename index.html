    Questions owners ask

    Do I need to know anything about AI or computers?

    No. If you can send a text, you can build a helper. You can even ask a family member to help through a link; they see the same screen you do.

    Will customers know they're talking to a helper?

    Yes, when it matters. Helpers that make calls say up front that they're an AI assistant working for your business. You choose the name and voice.

    What if it gets something wrong?

    It follows your house rules, gives "starting at" prices instead of guesses, and takes a message when it isn't sure. Every conversation is saved, and you can fix its behavior by just telling it.

    Can it move money or sign anything?

    Not without your yes. Bank connections are read-only. Payments go through links customers pay themselves, straight into your account.

    What does it cost?

    A flat monthly price per helper, with calls, texts and emails included up to a generous limit. No credits to count. (Pricing is still being designed for this prototype.)
